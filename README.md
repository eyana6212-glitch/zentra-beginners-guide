# Supplying and Borrowing on ZENTRA, Step by Step

*A beginner's guide to your first lending loop — written for someone who has never supplied or borrowed in DeFi before.*

Lending in DeFi has a reputation for being complicated — wallets, extensions, jargon, a dozen tabs open at once. ZENTRA shrinks all of that into one app, with lending markets, swapping, staking, and its own wallet built in. This guide walks you through the full loop — supply, borrow, repay, swap, and stake — one step at a time. No prior experience needed, and no step requires leaving the app.

One note first: this is a how-to, not financial advice. Rates move with supply and demand, and borrowing against collateral carries real risk. Start small, and never borrow more than you could comfortably repay.

## What ZENTRA Is

At its core, ZENTRA is a lending market. Holders supply assets so others can borrow them, and suppliers earn interest in return. Borrowers put up collateral of their own first. Around that core, the app adds a built-in swap — trade one asset for another without going elsewhere — and staking for its native token, $ZNT.

Everything lives under one roof: Dashboard shows your positions, Markets lists what you can supply and borrow, Swap handles trades, Stake handles $ZNT, and Quests walk you through the main actions. The assets you will see most often are CC, USDT, and ZNT.

## Step 1: Set Up Your Sky Wallet

You do not need an external crypto wallet. ZENTRA has its own wallet, the Sky Wallet, built into the app.

1. Create your ZENTRA account with your email and sign in.
2. Open the Wallet section and create your Sky Wallet inside the app — no extension to install, no seed phrase to guard.
3. Once the wallet exists, it holds your balances: CC, USDT, and ZNT.

Treat your ZENTRA login like any account that holds money: a strong password, never shared. The Sky Wallet is still labelled Beta, so keep amounts modest while you learn.

## Step 2: Supply an Asset

Supplying is the first half of the loop. You lend an asset into the market; borrowers pay interest on it, and that interest flows to suppliers as supply APY.

1. Go to Markets and pick an asset you hold — for example CC, USDT, or ZNT.
2. Choose Supply and enter the amount you want to put in. Start with a small amount on your first try.
3. Confirm the transaction. Your asset is now supplied, and it starts earning supply APY.

Two things to understand. First, **supply APY** is the annualised rate suppliers earn, and it is not fixed: it rises and falls with how much of the asset is being borrowed. The app shows the current rate before you confirm, so check it each time. Second, **your supplied assets become your collateral** — what lets you borrow in the next step, because the market holds them behind your loan.

You can withdraw supplied assets later, as long as they are not needed as collateral for an open loan — repay first, then withdraw.

## Step 3: Borrow Against Your Collateral

Borrowing is the second half. Because you have supplied assets, the market lets you borrow a different one — for example, supplying CC and borrowing USDT.

1. In Markets, pick the asset you want to borrow.
2. The app shows how much you can borrow against your collateral. Borrow well below the maximum — the gap is your safety margin, and it is what keeps your position safe if your collateral falls in value.
3. Enter the amount and confirm.

Borrowing is not free. You pay **borrow APY** — the annualised cost of the loan — which also moves with demand. Read the rate before you confirm, and remember interest accrues over time: a loan left open for weeks costs more than one closed in a day.

The risk to respect is liquidation. If your collateral drops far enough in value, the market can sell part of it to cover your loan. That is why the safety margin matters: borrowing a small fraction of the maximum leaves room for prices to move against you.

## Step 4: Repay Your Loan

Repaying closes the loop, and it is deliberately simple.

1. Go to your borrow position on the Dashboard or in Markets.
2. Choose Repay and decide whether to repay the whole loan or part of it. A partial repayment lowers what you owe and frees up some of your collateral.
3. Confirm. Once fully repaid, all your supplied assets are free again — leave them supplied to keep earning, or withdraw them.

Repay when you no longer need the funds — borrow interest keeps accruing while the loan is open.

## Step 5: Swap and Stake

With the lending loop under your belt, the other two features are quick to try.

**Swap.** Swap trades one asset for another inside the app — say, some $ZNT for CC. Pick what you are selling and buying, check the rate, confirm. The same action as swapping on any exchange, minus the trip to a different app.

**Stake $ZNT.** Staking means locking up your $ZNT to support the network and earn rewards in return. Go to Stake, choose the amount, confirm. Staked tokens are locked while staked, so only stake what you will not need short term, and read the unstaking terms before you commit.

## Three Habits Before You Borrow

- **Start small.** Your first supply and first borrow should be amounts you would not lose sleep over. The mechanics are the same at any size.
- **Watch your collateral.** Prices move. Check your positions after any big market move, and repay some of your loan if your safety margin is getting thin.
- **Read the rates every time.** Supply APY and borrow APY change with the market. The number you saw yesterday is not a promise about today.

## FAQ

**Do I need an external crypto wallet to use ZENTRA?**
No. The Sky Wallet is built into the app and created when you set up your account, so you can start without installing anything else.

**What happens if my collateral falls in value?**
Your borrowing power falls with it. If it falls far enough, the market may liquidate part of your collateral to repay your loan. Borrowing well below your maximum is the simplest way to keep that from happening.

**Can I withdraw my supplied assets at any time?**
Generally yes, as long as they are not locked as collateral behind an open loan. Repay the loan first, and the supplied assets become withdrawable again.

## Wrapping Up

That is the whole loop: supply, borrow, repay, and use swap and staking when you need them. No step is hard on its own — the trick is doing them in order, with small amounts, until the screens feel familiar.

Open ZENTRA, create your Sky Wallet, and try the first step with a small supply. The Quests page walks you through each action, and by the time you have finished them you will have done the full loop once — the fastest way to understand how lending on ZENTRA works.
